Tips for better captures

• Keep the phone stable and frame the entire projection.
• Tap Start camera and allow camera access. Use the rear camera if asked.
• Avoid people walking in front of the screen; movement can trigger a capture.
• Turn on Do Not Disturb and set Auto-Lock to Never temporarily.
• This works best when slides change cleanly. Animated content or moving pointers may create extra captures, so review thumbnails before exporting.
